Case study · Head Designer · Communiqué Ltd, Malta · 2021 – 2024

One idea, every medium: campaigns for Maltese brands

At Communiqué I led design for social, web and Google/Meta campaigns across more than 20 brands, from the billboard on the road to the post in the feed. Six campaigns show how a single idea travels through outdoor, print, social and web.

01 — HOW THE AGENCY WORKS

Brief, concept, every format

A campaign at Communiqué starts from a client brief and ends as a set of artworks for every medium the client buys: billboards and bus wraps, leaflets and brochures, Facebook and Instagram posts, web banners and short video. My job was to turn the concept into a system that holds across all of them, and to brief and review the interns who produced the adaptations.

A campaign is not a key visual. It is the same idea surviving a 12-metre billboard and a 1080-pixel square.

02 — FMCG

Pakkett French Fries: “Crunch”

Snack launch for Pakkett. The crunch is the product, so the crunch is the campaign: bold yellow and red, the fries breaking the frame, Maltese copy on the road and short punchy lines on mobile. Outdoor, social and a video layout.

Mr. Riley: Snack Attack art competition

A consumer activation for Mr. Riley snacks: an art competition promoted on social and a landing page, with a visual system that works for the call for entries, the “vote now” stage and the winners announcement.

04 — PUBLIC SECTOR

BICC Skill Card: a safety card for construction workers

Campaign for the Building Industry Consultative Council to get construction workers to apply for the Skill Card. The visual language borrows from the site itself: hazard-tape yellow and black, workers in hi-vis, the card as the hero. Billboard, Facebook, leaflet, poster, letter and newspaper ad.

RGF: “Take Action”

Awareness campaign for the Responsible Gaming Foundation. The “Take Action” sign stops you on a bus, on a brochure and in a feed; the website page layout, social posts and merchandising follow the same grid and colour blocks.

07 — ALWAYS-ON

Social content and the agency’s own voice

Beyond launches: testimonial series for NSC on Facebook, awards campaigns run on a content calendar (EPALE Malta Awards 2023), and Communiqué’s own print and recruitment pieces.

08 — WHAT I TOOK FROM IT

Three years, twenty-plus brands

This is where I moved from product design into marketing: learning to design for a media plan, to write a brief that an intern can execute, and to keep one idea consistent from a roadside billboard to a paid post. It is the ground under the campaigns I run today with measurement attached.
